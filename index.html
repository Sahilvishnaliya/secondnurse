    A deterministic engine calls the breach

    Hand mask overlapping the sterile tip for over 0.3s is a touch contamination. Cap outside the field is a field breach. Skipped steps are order breaches. Thresholds you can defend, events you can replay.

    ◆§ 03 · Anatomy

    Anatomy of a catch

    Four-tenths of a second, in four frames.

    t − 0.4s

    Approach

    The hand enters the bench. SAM holds its mask and identity; the tip’s mask is armed.

    t − 0.1s

    Proximity

    Masks near, not touching. No alert — proximity is not contamination, and false alarms teach people to ignore you.

    t 0.0s

    Overlap

    Hand mask ∩ tip mask crosses 40px². The dwell clock starts. One noisy frame still cannot fire.

    t + 0.3s

    The catch

    Sustained overlap confirmed. Procedure frozen, correction spoken, frames logged for the care team.

    Safety & scope

    What this is, and what it is not.

    It is a training system

    A physical training kit and a real procedure, watched live, with technique feedback for education. It detects the technique break a nurse trainer watches for — it cannot see bacteria, and it never claims to.

    A human stays in charge

    The system flags; a person decides. Every alert is reviewable with its frames, and session reports go to the care team. No diagnosis, no treatment decision, no autonomous clinical action.

    Private by construction

    Frames go only to the Meta Model API you configure, for segmentation. No patient data is collected or stored by SecondNurse.
