Introduction

Welcome to the SecondNurse documentation.

SecondNurse watches a person's hands while they perform a sterile procedure at home — a dialysis fluid exchange, a home infusion connection, catheter care — and stops them the second sterile technique breaks: a hand touching the sterile connector tip, a cap set down outside the sterile field, a step performed out of order.

A nurse trains a patient once. The patient then performs the procedure alone, at home, for years. The commonest cause of dialysis-related infection is “touch contamination” — a half-second contact nobody saw, nobody reported, discovered days later as peritonitis. SecondNurse is the watcher who is present for that second.

What is SecondNurse?

SecondNurse is built around one definition:

Contamination is a geometric event: a non-sterile mask overlapping a sterile mask, sustained over time.
  • Make the invisible visible. A touch you cannot see and may not feel becomes a measured, logged event.
  • Correct in the moment. Feedback arrives before the connection is made — not in a report after the infection.
  • Compile, don't hardcode. Any written protocol becomes executable checks.

Core philosophy

  1. Geometry over opinion: SAM 3.1 returns pixel masks. The breach decision is mask overlap sustained across frames — a measurement with thresholds, not a language model's guess.
  2. Protocol as code: Muse Wonder compiles a written procedure into a machine-checkable step graph. A new procedure is a new document, not a new codebase.
  3. Human in charge: The system flags; a person decides. Every alert is reviewable with frame evidence. SecondNurse does not diagnose, treat, or make clinical decisions.
  4. No invented results: There is no demo mode. Without a configured Meta API key, AI endpoints refuse with “configuration required” instead of fabricating output.

Who it is for

  • Patients and caregivers performing sterile procedures at home after discharge.
  • Training nurses and home-health programs who teach once and currently never observe again.
  • Builders who want a reference implementation of SAM 3.1 mask geometry driving real-time rules.

Start with Setup & First Watch, or jump to IoT Devices for the physical bench: cameras, mounts, sensors, and alert hardware.

Scope in one paragraph

SecondNurse is an educational training system used with a physical training kit. It detects technique breaks — the same events a nurse trainer watches for. It cannot see bacteria, does not diagnose infection, and is not a medical device. Clinical use on real patients would require recorded-trial validation and regulatory review.
